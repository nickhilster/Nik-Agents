Evidence system

Every claim has a provenance class.

Deep evidence is available, but never allowed to overwhelm the primary narrative.

Verified artifact

Repo, commit, file, deployed page, test, or recorded asset.

Agent statement

An agent describing what it directly handled or could inspect.

Human context

Nik supplying workflow context that is not visible in the artifacts.

Unresolved connection

A plausible handoff or relationship that the current record cannot prove.

Why preserve uncertainty?

Because the purpose is not to make every agent agree. The stronger proof is showing what each system could see, what it could not see, and how the record changed when another source challenged it.

The working principle

I treat AI products as components, not destinations.

The work is figuring out what each system can actually do, where it fails, what context it needs, what it costs, and how to combine it with people and other systems to make something real.
